Searched in the descriptions of the PDB sequences. Every structure with a chain whose sequence matches one of the hits is also downloaded.

Downloads the computed models that RCSB PDB finds for the keyword.

A computed model has one protein chain only: no nucleic acids, ligands, waters or other copies of the protein (e.g. a homodimer is modelled as a monomer). It covers the full UniProt sequence, signal peptides included, and its B-factor column holds pLDDT.

Best matches first. Leave blank to download all of them.

You will get an email when the job ends, with the job number and a link to the results. Look in your spam folder if it does not arrive. Your email address is deleted once the email is sent.

Advanced Options

Change some inputs and run again without downloading the structures again (the keyword and the computed model options must stay the same). The rerun is a new job, with its own job number; the earlier job is not changed. It starts from the earliest step whose input you changed, and it is deleted when the earlier job is.

Only the automatic step or an earlier one: a later step would ignore what you changed.

Residue columns present in less than this percentage of the structures are numbered with letters (insertion codes). Default 80.

One entity per line, ENTITY NAME:chainID,chainID,... (one chain ID per copy), e.g. TRIOSEPHOSPHATE ISOMERASE:A,Ab. Without it, the chains are named from the MMseqs2 clusters, and the generated input_file.txt is in the zip file: download it, edit it and upload it here to change the names.

A JSON file {"chain ID": "sequence", ...}, like the StandardSequences.json written by the chain-checking step (in standard_ChainID_bank/ of the zip file). Without it, the Standard Sequences are the consensus of all the structures.

Downloaded structures that should not be curated. Type them in the box and/or upload a text file: one or more per line, separated by spaces or commas (# starts a comment). Give a file name of clean_bank/ (pdb_00001abc+01.cif, with or without .cif), or a PDB ID (1abc) or entry (pdb_00001abc, AF-P00780-F1) to leave out all its assemblies. They stay in clean_bank/, and excluded_structures.txt in the zip file lists what was left out and the names that matched nothing. Without a list, all the structures are curated.

You get a job number: it is the only way to find your job again, and anyone who has it can view the job. The job and its results are deleted 7 days after it ends.
